★ Stargazing — Sutherland Planetarium
All 6 people. Sutherland sits at 1,450 m (4,760 ft) above sea level under one of the darkest skies in the southern hemisphere. The Milky Way is visible to the naked eye. About 2 hours under the stars with telescope access.

Dress properly warm — thermals, jacket, gloves and hat. It is genuinely cold out there.

Contact: WhatsApp +27 78 603 0058
BOOKED · 6 people R175 / person

Stargazing tips

  • Eyes take 20 min to dark-adapt — no phone screens at the site
  • Dress warmer than you think you need to
  • May skies are stable and dry — conditions are excellent
  • The Milky Way core is visible this time of year

After stargazing

  • Blue Moon restaurant closes early — plan ahead
  • OK Food Store for provisions if needed
  • Skitterland breakfast is included — sleep in peace
